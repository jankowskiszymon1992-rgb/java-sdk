The user initially requested to transform a generic boilerplate into an application for an electrician. This evolved into the Elektron app with several key features:

**Core Management:**
-   Client management: List, add, edit, delete clients with addresses and phones.
-   Project management: List projects with clients, status (New, In Progress, Completed), start/end dates, descriptions.
-   Work hour registration: An Excel-like table for recording work hours (date, hours, project, notes), with weekly/monthly views and summing.

**Mobile & Desktop (PWA):**
-   Installable on Android and Windows, acting as a native app.
-   Real-time data synchronization between devices.
-   Offline functionality: Works without internet, saves locally, synchronizes upon reconnection.
-   Push notifications for reminders.

**Branding:**
-   Application name: Elektron.
-   Integration of a user-provided company logo in the header, PWA icons, and favicon.

**Material Notes:**
-   A Notatka (Notes) feature on the Zlecenia (Projects) page.
-   An editable, Excel-like table within a modal for tracking materials: Zużyte (Used), Do nabycia (To Acquire), Do oddania (To Return), with categories, quantity, unit, notes, and a summary.

**Calculators:**
-   Basic mathematical calculator.
-   Specialized electrical calculators: Wire selection, voltage drop, fuse selection, Ohm's law, power calculation.
-   A section for electrical tips and norms.
-   All calculators presented as separate, distinct cards for improved usability.

**Photos:**
-   A Zdjęcia (Photos) page to document work with photos.
-   Functionality to take new photos or upload from a gallery.
-   Ability to assign photos to projects and add descriptions.
