<product_requirements>
The Elektron application initially provided tools for electricians, including client/project management, work hour tracking, PWA features (offline, real-time sync, push notifications), custom branding, material tracking, calculators, and photo documentation. A voice reporting system was also in place.

User requests expanded significantly to include:
1.  **Personal AI Assistant:** Core chat and voice functionalities initially, later evolved to include financial analysis, OCR, automated payroll, smart purchasing, and e-commerce (though the e-commerce part was split into a separate app).
2.  **Employee System:** Manage helper staff's work hours and calculate earnings, integrated into the main Elektron application.
3.  **Detailed Financial Management System:** OCR for purchase/sales invoices and fuel receipts (gross/net), nine categories for entries, a main table view with gross/net summaries, Excel/PDF export, and yearly income/expense charts.
4.  **Reminders System:** Monthly recurring payments (ZUS, taxes) and custom notes, with reliable push notifications to the phone.
5.  **New Elektron Market Intelligence Application:** (Requested as a separate app)
    *   **Price Monitoring:** Web scraping from electrical wholesalers/stores (Kanlux, TME, Conrad, RS Components, etc.) for competitor price comparison and alerts.
    *   **Sales Trend Analysis:** Identify best-selling products, predict demand, and leverage historical data with ML.
    *   **USD Exchange Rate Monitoring:** Track USD/PLN, correlate with electrical cable prices (due to copper), and provide alerts/predictions.
    *   **AI Analyst:** An intelligent AI agent (using Claude Sonnet 4 and GPT-5) to analyze market data, generate detailed reports, provide buying/pricing recommendations (e.g., KUP TERAZ / CZEKAJ), and offer an interactive chat interface with full read/write access to all application data (Finances, Orders, Reports, Work Hours, Employees).
    *   **Chat History:** For both AI agents, with editable titles and the ability to resume conversations.
</product_requirements>
